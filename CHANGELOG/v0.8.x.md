## v0.8.3 — Invitation System + Role Hierarchy
*Released: May 21, 2026*

### 🔐 Auth & Roles
- Added **Discord-gated registration** — new accounts require Discord role verification
- Added full role hierarchy: `FOUNDER` (super-admin) → `MODERATOR` (read-only admin) → `SUPPORT` (no panel) → `USER`

- System role badge shown on public profile

- Full invitation system: invite links, expiry, join flow

- Public player profiles with locked sidebar

- Fixed name history popover overflow on logo card

- Fixed `requireAuth` on `/:id/timeline` route
